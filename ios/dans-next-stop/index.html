The small print, made small.

Times are planning estimates, not live departures or a ±10-minute promise. Peak traffic, missed connections and conditions can add more than 10 minutes. The return switch reverses the route; it does not look up a new timetable.

Public transport: starts or ends at Point Clare Station. Waiting is extra unless a transfer allowance is explicitly included. A time labelled “+ access” is only the timed part of the journey.

Drive: starts or ends in Point Clare. Parking and the final walk are extra. Road ranges use published baselines where available, nearby-area proxies or local planning estimates; the route details state which.

Own boat: starts or ends at the user-described Point Clare ramp. These are the original map’s modelled water legs, including the displayed intermediate waypoints, not a verified fastest direct passage. Model: up to 20 knots offshore, 8 knots on the lake / Brisbane Water, slower harbour zones and 4-knot approaches. Launching and loading are extra.

A few places need the exact pin.

Point Clare ramp is the former-wharf / walkway site; its exact pin remains unconfirmed. Palm Beach is the Pittwater side. Kirribilli uses Holbrook Avenue Wharf, near Royal Sydney Yacht Squadron. Bayview Park is Burwood Road, Concord.

Leichhardt’s restaurant wharf is waiting for a name. Beach landings, private club access and public-wharf berths need checking. There is no dependable lake-to-ocean route through The Entrance bar in this guide.
